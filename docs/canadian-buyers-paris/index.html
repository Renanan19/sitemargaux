Short answers

Can Canadians buy property in France?

Yes. France places no nationality restriction on buying residential property, and you do not need to live in France to buy.

How long can a Canadian stay in France without a visa?

Up to 90 days in any 180-day period in the Schengen area. Owning an apartment does not extend that. Longer stays need a French long-stay visa.

Do you work in French as well as English?

Yes. Margaux is French. Clients from Québec and francophone Canada work with her in French; everyone else in English.

Do I have to travel for the purchase?

Not necessarily. The final deed can be signed by power of attorney. Most buyers come once to see the apartment before committing.

Tell Margaux what you want in Paris.

Budget, neighbourhood if you have one, and whether you plan to live in it or rent it. She replies in English.
