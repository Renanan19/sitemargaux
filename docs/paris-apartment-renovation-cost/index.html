Stouls Studio / Renovation cost

What does it cost to renovate an apartment in Paris?

Paris firms publish ranges from about €500 per m² (€46 per sq ft) for a refresh to €3,500 per m² (€325 per sq ft) for a premium renovation. The state of the wiring, plumbing and structure moves the figure more than the floor area does.

Stouls Studio, Paris. Updated November 15, 2026.

These are figures published by other Paris firms, quoted here so you can compare apples to apples. They are not Stouls Studio quotes. I price the apartment in front of me.

Take 65 m² (700 sq ft), the size of the median purchase by an American non-resident buyer in Paris in 2025 (Notaires du Grand Paris). A complete renovation at €1,200 to €2,000 per m² comes to €78,000 to €130,000 before design fees and furniture.

Why do two apartments of the same size cost such different amounts?

  • Wiring. Old wiring has to be replaced entirely, which means opening walls and ceilings.
  • Plumbing. Moving a kitchen or adding a bathroom depends on where the building’s waste pipes run.
  • Structure. Opening a load-bearing wall needs an engineer, steel, and a vote of the copropriété (the building’s co-ownership).
  • Floors. Old parquet can be restored, or may hide joists that need work.
  • Windows. Replacing them changes the energy rating and needs the building’s and the city’s agreement.
  • Access. A seventh floor (6th floor in French counting, where the ground floor is zero) without an elevator costs more in labor for everything carried up and down.
  • Finish. Custom millwork, stone and fixtures can double a budget on their own.

What comes on top of the contractor’s quote?

  • Design and site supervision fees.
  • Furniture, lighting, curtains and linens.
  • Engineer’s study, when structure is touched.
  • Fees charged by the syndic (the building’s managing agent) for handling an approval.
  • A contingency. In an old building, something is always found behind a wall.

Short answers

How much does it cost to renovate an apartment in Paris?

Paris firms publish ranges of about €500 to €900 per m² (€46 to €84 per sq ft) for a refresh, €1,200 to €2,000 per m² (€111 to €186 per sq ft) for a complete renovation and €2,000 to €3,500 per m² (€186 to €325 per sq ft) for a premium one. What the wiring, plumbing and structure need counts for more than the size.

What is that in square feet?

One square meter is 10.76 square feet. €1,500 per m² is about €139 per square foot.

What is not in a contractor’s quote?

Design fees, furniture, kitchen appliances in some quotes, building-approval costs, and a contingency for what is found when walls are opened.

How long does a renovation take?

It depends on scope and on building approvals. Work that needs a vote of the copropriété cannot start before the general meeting that approves it.
