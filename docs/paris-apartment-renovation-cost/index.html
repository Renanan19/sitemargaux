What does it cost to renovate an apartment in Paris?

Paris firms publish ranges from about €500 per m² for a refresh to €3,500 per m² or more for a premium renovation. The state of the wiring, plumbing and structure moves the figure more than the floor area does.

On the median foreign purchase in Paris, 65 m² (Notaires du Grand Paris), a complete renovation at €1,200 to €2,000 per m² comes to €78,000 to €130,000 before design fees and furniture.

Why do two apartments of the same size cost so differently?

  • Wiring. Old installations have to be replaced entirely, which means opening walls and ceilings.
  • Plumbing. Moving a kitchen or adding a bathroom depends on where the building's waste pipes run.
  • Structure. Opening a load-bearing wall needs an engineer, steel, and the co-owners' vote.
  • Floors. Old parquet can be restored, or may hide joists that need work.
  • Windows. Replacing them changes the energy rating and needs the building's and the city's agreement.
  • Access. A sixth floor without an elevator costs more in labor for everything carried up and down.
  • Finish. Custom millwork, stone and fittings can double a budget on their own.

What comes on top of the contractor's quote?

  • Design and site-management fees.
  • Furniture, lighting, curtains and linen.
  • Engineer's study, when structure is touched.
  • Fees charged by the building manager for handling an approval.
  • A contingency. In an old building, something is always found behind a wall.

Short answers

How much does it cost to renovate an apartment in Paris?

Paris firms publish ranges from about €500 to €900 per m² for a refresh, €1,200 to €2,000 per m² for a complete renovation and €2,000 to €3,500 per m² or more for a premium one. The state of the wiring, plumbing and structure matters more than the floor area.

What is that in square feet?

One square meter is 10.76 square feet. €1,500 per m² is about €139 per square foot.

What is not in a contractor's quote?

Design fees, furniture, kitchen appliances in some quotes, building-approval costs, and a contingency for what is found when walls are opened.

How long does a renovation take?

It depends on scope and on building approvals. Works that need a vote of the co-owners cannot start before the general meeting that approves them.
