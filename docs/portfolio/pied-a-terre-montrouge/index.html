Pied-à-terre, Montrouge

A 46 m² pied-à-terre at the southern edge of Paris, fully renovated, bright and full of colour.

Type
Pied-à-terre
Location
Montrouge, south of Paris
Surface
About 46 m² (495 sq ft)
Work by the studio
Full renovation

The living room is organised along one wall: a long run of white cupboards with an oak top carries the television and an inset fireplace, with shelves for books above. The dining table stands by the entrance to the room, on a bench with storage that runs along the wall.

The kitchen is a galley in deep green, with a solid wood worktop, a green glazed splashback and a black-and-white patterned floor. In the hallway, a desk and its shelves are fitted into a niche painted mustard yellow.

The bedroom has a wall of dark botanical wallpaper behind the bed and a white chest of drawers and wardrobe built in beside the window. The shower room is tiled in sage green from floor to ceiling.
