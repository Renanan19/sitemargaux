Apartment, Boulogne-Billancourt

Black cabinets, warm wood and an old pine floor in an apartment on the edge of Paris.

Type
Apartment renovation
Location
Boulogne-Billancourt, west of Paris
Work by the studio
Kitchen, bathroom and built-in joinery

The kitchen is open to the living room and drawn as one piece of furniture with it: matt black cabinets, worktops in warm solid wood, a splashback and floor in dark speckled stone. The wood climbs the wall and crosses the ceiling above the breakfast bar, and comes back down as a bench under the window, with drawers below and a cushion on top.

The living room keeps what was there: the wide pine floorboards, the black marble fireplace and its gilt mirror. Beside the fireplace, pale green cupboards and a wooden shelf frame the television, above a low black cabinet with leather pulls.

A desk in light wood fits an alcove next to an arched niche painted the same pale green. In the bathroom, the shower is lined in the dark stone of the kitchen and screened by glass blocks, against white bevelled tiles, a round mirror and a pale wood vanity.
