House on Sifnos

A jazz club that everyone on the island knew, turned into a 150 m² holiday house.

Type
Holiday house
Location
Sifnos, Greece
Surface
About 150 m² (1,615 sq ft)
Work by the studio
Full conversion of a former jazz club into a house

The building had been a jazz club, well known on Sifnos. It came with twelve toilets and professional kitchens. Everything was rethought to make it a home.

The original floor was kept, cleaned and restored. The project holds on to the Greek and Sifnian character of the place and to the memory of the club, with a plain approach: lime on the walls and local materials, above all the island's own stone.

Inside, the living room sits under a painted beamed ceiling with an arched doorway, and the oak kitchen runs along one wall under a plastered hood, on star-patterned cement tiles. A corridor paved in the island's stone leads to the bedrooms, and the bathroom is finished in a smooth sand-coloured plaster, shower included.
