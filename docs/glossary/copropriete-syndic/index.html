What are a copropriété and a syndic?

A copropriété is a building owned in shares by its apartment owners, the French equivalent of a condominium. The syndic is the manager they appoint to run it.

Stouls Studio, Paris. Updated 11 November 2026.

How does a copropriété work?

When you buy an apartment in Paris you buy a lot: your private unit plus a fixed share of everything that is shared. That share sets your vote at meetings and your part of the bills.

French termWhat it is
Règlement de copropriétéThe building's rule book: what is private, what is shared, what uses are allowed.
SyndicThe manager, usually a professional firm, appointed by vote.
Conseil syndicalA few owners elected to oversee the syndic.
Assemblée généraleThe owners' meeting, at least once a year, where everything is voted.
ChargesRunning costs, billed quarterly according to your share.
Travaux votésWorks already approved, such as a new roof, that owners will be billed for.

What should a buyer read before making an offer?

  • The minutes of the last three general meetings. They show disputes, unpaid charges and works under discussion.
  • The works voted but not yet billed, and who pays them: seller or buyer.
  • The annual charges for the unit.
  • The rule book, for limits on use, such as short lets or a professional practice.

The seller's agent or notaire supplies these. They are in French, and they are where a cheap apartment turns out to be an expensive one.

What does the copropriété decide about a renovation?

Inside your own walls, with nothing shared touched, you need no vote. As soon as the work affects load-bearing walls, floors, shared pipes, the façade or the windows, the owners must approve it at a general meeting (Service-Public).

Short answers

What is a copropriété?

A copropriété is a building divided into privately owned units, where each owner also holds a share of the common parts such as the roof, stairs and façade. It is the French equivalent of a condominium.

What is a syndic?

The syndic is the manager appointed by the co-owners to run the building: it collects charges, pays the bills, keeps the accounts, organises repairs and calls the annual general meeting.

What are charges de copropriété?

The building's running costs, shared among owners according to their share: cleaning, insurance, lift, the syndic's fee, sometimes heating and water. They are billed quarterly.

How often do co-owners meet?

At least once a year, at the general meeting, where the accounts, the budget and any works are voted.

Do I need the copropriété's permission to renovate?

Not for work inside your unit that touches nothing shared. Work affecting load-bearing walls, floors, shared pipes or the façade needs a vote at the general meeting.
