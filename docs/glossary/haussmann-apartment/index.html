What is a Haussmann apartment?

A Haussmann apartment is a flat in one of the cut-stone buildings put up during the rebuilding of Paris led by Georges-Eugène Haussmann between 1853 and 1870, and in the same style until about 1914.

Stouls Studio, Paris. Updated 11 November 2026.

How do you recognise a Haussmann building?

  • A façade in pale cut stone, lined up with its neighbours.
  • Five or six storeys under a grey zinc or slate roof.
  • A continuous balcony on the second floor and another on the fifth.
  • Shops at street level and a low floor just above them.
  • Maids' rooms under the roof. What a chambre de bonne is.

What changes when one is renovated?

The layout was drawn for a household with staff. The kitchen is far from the dining room, there is one bathroom for four bedrooms, and a fifth of the floor area is corridor.

  • The kitchen moves to the front, beside or inside the living room.
  • The old kitchen becomes a bedroom or a bathroom, since the pipes are already there.
  • Part of the corridor is absorbed into rooms or closets.
  • Wiring and plumbing are replaced throughout.
  • Mouldings, parquet and fireplaces are kept and repaired.

Short answers

What is a Haussmann apartment?

Is it Haussmann or Haussmannian?

Both are used in English. Haussmannian is the adjective, from the French haussmannien. They describe the same buildings.

Which floor is best in a Haussmann building?

The second floor was the grandest, with the highest ceilings and a continuous balcony. Today the fifth floor, which also has a balcony and more light, is often the most sought after when there is a lift.

Can you remove walls in a Haussmann apartment?

Some. Many internal walls carry load or brace the building. Any change to a load-bearing wall needs a structural engineer and a vote of the co-owners.

Can you add air conditioning?

It is decided building by building. Outdoor units on a façade or roof need the building's approval and usually a filing with the city, and are refused on many protected streets.
