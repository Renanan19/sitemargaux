Your personal data

This site sets no cookies, runs no analytics and has no contact form. It does not collect anything about you while you read it.

When you write to me

If you send a message by WhatsApp, email or Instagram, I receive what you choose to send: your name, your contact details and what you say about your project. I use it only to answer you and, if you go ahead, to carry out the work. It is not sold or passed on for marketing.

Messages are kept for as long as the exchange or the project lasts, then for the period French law requires for business records.

Services that receive technical data

  • GitHub, the host, receives your IP address when your browser loads a page, as any web server does.
  • Google Fonts supplies the typefaces. Your browser requests them from Google, which receives your IP address.
  • WhatsApp and Instagram are run by Meta. Their own terms apply once you follow those links.

Your rights

Under the EU General Data Protection Regulation you can ask to see, correct or delete the information I hold about you, or object to its use. Write to contact@stoulsstudio.com. You can also complain to the French data protection authority, the CNIL.

The guides and glossary give general information, checked against the official sources linked on each page on the date shown. They are not legal, tax or financial advice. Rules change: confirm with a notaire, lawyer or tax adviser before you act.
