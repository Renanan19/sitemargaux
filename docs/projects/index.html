Projects by Margaux Stouls

Homes, rental apartments and furniture, with the visualisations and drawings behind them.

Main residencesSecond homesRental propertiesCustom-made furniture3D visualisationsTechnical drawings

Main residences

Apartments people live in all year, in Paris and the towns around it. Kitchens opened to the living room, bathrooms rebuilt, storage built in, and the old timber posts, fireplaces and floors kept.

Second homes

Houses used for part of the year, designed for owners who are not there to supervise: a country house in France and a house on the Greek island of Sifnos.

Rental properties

Small apartments built to be let furnished. Every square metre is given a job: a kitchen along one wall, a desk under a skylight, a bed on a mezzanine, a bench that is also storage.

Custom-made furniture

Joinery and furniture drawn for one room and made by cabinetmakers: wardrobes, libraries, headboards, sideboards and tables.

3D visualisations

Before work starts, rooms and furniture are modelled in three dimensions. A client abroad sees the result on screen and can change it while changing still costs nothing.

Technical drawings

The drawings contractors build from: plans for electrics, lighting and plumbing, and dimensioned elevations of every piece of joinery.
