Stouls Studio / Projects

Apartments designed by Margaux Stouls

Five homes in Paris and just outside it, photographed once the works were finished.

Message Margaux on WhatsAppInstagram

Apartment, Paris 9th

An apartment in the 9th arrondissement, redesigned around its original timber posts. Openings were redrawn as arches, the bedroom and bathroom wear the same deep green to half height, and the storage is built in behind cane-fronted doors. Herringbone oak runs through every room.

Studio with mezzanine, Paris 6th

A small apartment under old exposed beams in the 6th arrondissement. The bed went up onto a glazed mezzanine, the stair to reach it holds the television and the storage, and the living room got its floor back.

Top-floor studio

A studio under the roof, laid out as a real one-room home: a black kitchen set against the old brick, a desk under the skylight, a sleeping corner with its own storage, and a compact tiled shower room.

Apartment, Boulogne-Billancourt

A black kitchen opened onto the living room, with a window seat built into its run. The marble fireplace and gilt mirror stayed. A desk was fitted into an arched niche.

Apartment, Colombes

A full wall of library and cupboards in the living room, a blush kitchen on a cabochon floor, deep teal panelling down the corridor, and a marble vanity in the bathroom.

What these have in common

  • Storage is built in, drawn to the centimetre for rooms that are never square.
  • What the building already had is kept: beams, fireplaces, mouldings, old oak floors.
  • Colour goes on joinery and in small rooms, where it costs little and changes most.
  • Kitchens and bathrooms are redrawn first, because they decide where everything else can go.
